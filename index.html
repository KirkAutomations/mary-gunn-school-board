Meet Mary

A calm hand and a clear standard.

Mary's career started in healthcare and leadership. She has spent decades making hard calls, teaching future nurses, and helping teams stay composed when stakes are high.

  • Madonna University Bachelor of Science in Nursing
  • RN since 1984
  • Beaumont/Corewell Health leadership
  • Baker College and Oakland University nursing instructor
Why it matters

Her background is practical. It is leadership under pressure, compassion with boundaries, and a habit of turning hard problems into clear decisions.

Priorities

Five commitments for the board room.

The work is practical. The standard is simple.

Academic excellence

Evidence-based reading, strong math, clear expectations, and room for arts, career, and technical success.

Students first

Safe, orderly schools

Secure buildings, consistent discipline, emergency readiness, and calm communication when it matters most.

Safety matters

Parents as partners

Clear communication, transparent decisions, and more than a dashboard when families need answers.

Transparency

Teachers empowered
